# Summary

Feedback describes what happened after the system attempted to realize intent. Source revision, apply results, readiness and user-facing health answer different questions. Agents can use feedback for further control decisions, while operators need clear errors and freshness. A successful write or a green process does not establish every downstream outcome. Feedback should preserve uncertainty and identify the contract needing attention rather than flattening all conditions into one success label.
