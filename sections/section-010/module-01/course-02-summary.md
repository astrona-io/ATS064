# Summary

Continuous operation means the management process keeps returning to its responsibility over time. It does not require instantaneous observation or correction. Events can reduce delay, while periodic or recoverable evaluation supports missed-event handling. A one-time successful deployment is narrower evidence than an operating control process. Intervals, permissions, failure policy and available capacity determine whether repeated evaluation can make useful progress toward the intended state.
